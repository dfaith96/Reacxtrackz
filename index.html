Platform Structure

Three simple layers for clearer treatment review.

Log

Capture what happened.

Patients record symptoms, side effects, medication names, dose timing, and severity in one structured flow.

Compare

Connect events over time.

Entries become a timeline that shows whether reactions are improving, repeating, or becoming harder to tolerate.

Review

Prepare better conversations.

Care teams get cleaner context for follow-up decisions instead of relying only on memory at the appointment.

Why It Matters

Reactions rarely tell a clean story in one visit.

Reacxtrackz keeps the small details together: when symptoms started, how severe they became, what changed after rest or hydration, and whether the same pattern returned after the next dose.
